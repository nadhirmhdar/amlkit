# What's new in groAML

## 1 November 2026

- Billing: self-serve subscriptions (Starter, Professional, Consultant) with plan limits, available once billing is switched on for your workspace.
- Billing page shows operator and customer usage against your plan, with a six-month history of customers onboarded and operators added.
- Dashboard first-run checklist walks a new workspace through its first steps and ticks them off automatically.
- Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) to search customers, alerts, reports and pages from anywhere.

## 31 October 2026

- Web hygiene: private pages are kept out of search engines, security headers tightened, clearer session and password messages, and updated privacy wording.
- Governance: a reason is required when a risk rating is downgraded, false-positive notes are recorded, sanctions refreshes show who ran them, and UAE PASS identity checks report whether they agree with the customer file.
- The Google-based auxiliary screeners were removed; sanctions screening is unchanged.
- Paging, zero-threshold and malformed-input handling hardened across lists, freeze notifications, goAML XML export, transaction monitoring and search.
- Periodic CDD review now has an explicit review action and a record of each review; other re-assessments no longer restart the review clock, and overdue reviews are badged.
- Screening correctness: blank and invisible-character names are handled, names that cannot be screened are kept and flagged, related parties are fully re-screened, and sector values are validated.
- Uploads: one shared guard for size, type and image dimensions on every scan and document route, with unique stored file names.
- One password and identity validation rule now applies at every entry point, with rate limits on mobile sign-in.
- Freezes are limited to UN and UAE local list matches, freeze obligations can be resolved with controls, and one-time secrets can be used only once.
- Fixed a persistent sign-in lockout, and MLROs can now unlock a locked operator.
