What groAML covers

The day-to-day AML/CFT work of a small or mid-sized regulated firm, in one place.

Sanctions and PEP screening

Screen individuals and entities against the UAE Local Terrorist List, UN, OFAC, EU and UK sanctions lists and politically exposed person data. Arabic-aware matching handles transliteration variants and both Latin and Arabic script.

CDD and KYC

One case file per customer with beneficial ownership, supporting documents and evidence. {% if uaepass_live %}Verify identity with UAE PASS and capture{% else %}Capture{% endif %} details from Emirates ID and passport scans.

Risk assessment and periodic review

A configurable risk model scores each customer, and a review clock keeps track of when the next periodic review is due. Transaction and adverse media checks feed back into the file.

goAML reporting and freeze obligations

Prepare STR/SAR reports from the case file and export goAML 5.0 XML. Confirmed matches on the UN Security Council and UAE Local Terrorist lists create freeze obligations that are tracked until they are executed.

How it works

  1. OnboardCreate the customer, add owners and documents, and verify identity.
  2. ScreenCheck names against sanctions and PEP lists, then work through any alerts.
  3. AssessScore the customer, record the rationale and set the review date.
  4. ReportEscalate to the MLRO, file through goAML and keep the evidence on record.

Built to keep your records separate and traceable

Separate workspace per firm

Every query is scoped to your organisation, so one firm's customers and screenings are never visible to another.

Append-only audit trail

Actions are recorded in an audit log that the database prevents from being edited or deleted.

MFA for MLROs

MLRO accounts must complete two-factor authentication, with backup codes and lockout on repeated failures.
