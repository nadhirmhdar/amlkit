UAE AML/CFT obligations for DNFBPs

An overview of who is covered, the duties that apply, and where groAML fits. General information, not legal advice.

Who is obliged

The UAE AML/CFT framework applies to financial institutions and to designated non-financial businesses and professions (DNFBPs). DNFBPs include real-estate brokers and agents, dealers in precious metals and stones, auditors and accountants, lawyers and notaries, and corporate service providers. Your sector supervisor sets out how the framework applies to your activity.
