Privacy & Cookies

How amlkit uses cookies and data

This page describes amlkit's actual practices. There is nothing here that does not happen, and nothing that happens is left out.

Cookies we set

amlkit sets three cookies, all strictly necessary for the site to function. None are used for advertising, analytics, or tracking across other sites, and none are shared with third parties.

amlkit_session
Keeps you signed in. HttpOnly (not readable by page scripts) and SameSite=Strict. Expires when you log out or your session times out.
amlkit_csrf
Protects forms and actions from cross-site request forgery. HttpOnly. Present even before you sign in, since the login and registration forms need it too.
amlkit_flash
Carries a one-time success or error message across a redirect (for example, after saving a form). HttpOnly, expires after 60 seconds or as soon as the message is shown.
What we don't do

amlkit does not run advertising or ad-tracking cookies, does not use Google Analytics or similar third-party analytics scripts, does not sell or share data with advertisers, and does not participate in any ad-industry opt-out program (there is nothing to opt out of). The only outbound network calls a page makes are to amlkit's own servers and, where a firm has explicitly enabled a specific optional feature (such as AI-assisted screening or drafting), to the named third-party API that powers it — never to an ad network.

Compliance data

Beyond cookies, amlkit processes the customer due diligence, screening, and transaction data your organisation enters, strictly to provide the AML/CFT compliance functions you use it for: sanctions and PEP screening, risk assessment, transaction monitoring, and regulatory reporting. That data is kept separate per organisation, is retained for the period required by UAE AML law, and is never used for advertising or shared with unrelated third parties.
