## What the team missed
1. **The sign-up change reopened an enumeration channel the login path was built to close.** Four roles looked at SS1's neighbours (payment gate, MFA, rate limit) and only red-team flagged the "already exists" message. The deliberate generic-error design in `auth.login` should apply to registration too. Same family as LG2 and CR-34.
2. **The two sign-up doors now behave differently** (web open, mobile invite-gated) and nobody checked whether the mobile one was meant to stay closed in Stripe mode. If it is a deliberate carve-out, say so in CLAUDE.md; if not, it is dead or hidden surface.
3. **Marketing claims are now part of the audit surface.** UAE PASS (MK1/CS-24) and freeze scope (MK2/CS-25) are two separate public statements that the product does not fully back. A short "claims checklist" run against `landing.html`, `aml_law.html` and `pricing.html` at each content change would catch this earlier than the daily loop.
4. **Interpreter drift is invisible until a container rebuild.** The jump from Python 3.11 to 3.13 and FastAPI 0.142 to 0.162 broke QA's and red-team's route enumeration last week and cost every role a venv rebuild today. The Dockerfile's Python version is what production runs; none of the daily environments has been checked against it. Worth one line in the deploy checklist.
5. **Stripe go-live (#458) is the first change whose real behaviour nobody can test.** Red-team and code-reviewer tested the gate with a dummy key; the Checkout success path, the trial transition and annual prices need Stripe's test mode and were not exercised by any role. That is a deploy-time check, not a daily one.
