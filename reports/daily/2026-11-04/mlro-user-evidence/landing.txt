Sanctions and PEP screening Screen individuals and entities against the UAE Local Terrorist List, UN, OFAC, EU and UK sanctions lists and politically exposed person data. Arabic-aware matching handles transliteration variants and both Latin and Arabic script. CDD and KYC One case file per customer with beneficial ownership, supporting documents and evidence. Verify identity with UAE PASS and capture details from Emirates ID and passport scans. Risk assessment and periodic review A configurable risk model scores each customer, and a review clock keeps track of when the next periodic review is due. Transaction and adverse media checks feed back into the file. goAML reporting and freeze obligations Prepare STR/SAR reports from the case file and export goAML 5.0 XML. Confirmed sanctions matches create freeze obligations that are tracked until they are executed. How it works Onboard Create the customer, add owners and documents, and verify identity. Screen Check names against sanctions and PEP lists, then work through any alerts. Assess Score the customer, record the rationale and set the review date. Report Escalate to the MLRO, file through goAML and keep the evidence on record.
