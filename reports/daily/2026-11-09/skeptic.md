## What the team missed
1. **RT1 and 3.1 are one gap seen from two sides.** Compliance tracks the AED 55,000 threshold as "organisation-configurable"; mlro-user tracks the missing DPMSR/REAR reports. Together they mean the product both lets a firm change the number that triggers a duty and cannot produce the report that duty ends in. Neither role connected them, and the monitor's own comment names the link.
2. **SS3b and SS5 combine into a phishing channel, not two nuisances.** Red-team rated both Low separately. Together, an anonymous visitor can send an attacker-written message with a trusted sender and a real link to any address, rate-limited only per IP. The two fixes (cooldown and a name restriction) are small and both belong in the same change as the timing fix (CR-41), since all three are the open sign-up form.
3. **Three roles listed "tesseract, GDELT, goAML XSD" as not checked for weeks.** None of the daily environments has the FIU schema, so every goAML claim (including the sector report codes mlro-user could not validate) rests on code reading. A single committed XSD, or a note that the FIU does not publish one, would remove the standing caveat.
4. **Pricing and the public site have not been checked against RT1.** After #462 aligned the freeze and UAE PASS claims with the product, the same exercise should be run for "goAML reporting": which report types, for which sectors.
